Important status and exclusions

This invoice schedule describes the current website implementation. It does not include the Odda View customer dashboard, authentication, IoT services, a database, CMS, ecommerce, subscription checkout, GoCardless API integration, legal drafting, hosting fees or third-party subscriptions.

Before production launch, Odda Care must confirm the real contact email, legal-policy and login destinations, social links, hosting, analytics/cookie requirements and final acceptance testing. The contact form currently uses the visitor’s email application rather than a server-side form service.
